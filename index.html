Engineering Methodology & Formulations
  • Structural Code Minimum vs. Runway Practices: The building's structural slab mandates an absolute minimum spread width Bcode, min = 2h (300 mm for a 150 mm slab) to transition punching shear into Zone 3 flexure. In practical transit, riggers use wider runway sheets (e.g., 610 mm or 1220 mm wide) so skates do not roll off the edge, which also significantly reduces the manual labor of leapfrogging small plates.
  • Mode 1 (2 Powered Skates Only): Baseline benchmark. Total gross load is divided equally by 2 (50% each = 40.2 kN nominal). The powered skates are positioned at the center of the 2.20 m ends. Without outriggers, high roller loads (> 10 kN) cause severe slab punching risk.
  • Mode 2 (6-Point Perimeter Rig): 2 powered drive units (P1, P2) located at the center of the 2.20 m ends + 4 corner follower skids. Symmetrical nominal share is 1/6 each (13.5 kN). Floor rocking dynamic peak assumes 25% × 1.20 = 24.4 kN on the heaviest pivot corner, with 50% wheel ground contact.
  • Mode 3 (Diamond 4-Point Rig): 2 powered units (P1, P2) at the 2.20 m ends + 2 centerline swivel bogies (F1, F2) on the 3.80 m sides. Nominal share is 1/4 (20.3 kN). Deceleration pitching applies 35% × 1.20 = 34.1 kN peak. Enables 360° zero-turn rotation without floor scrubbing.
  • Spreader Plate Cantilever Mechanics (AISC Design Guide 1): Overhang cantilever distance c = (B − Wskate) / 2. Required plate thickness treq = c √(3p / σallow). If t < treq, the plate curls upward (potato chip), causing edge lift-off and concentrating the entire load as an illegal point punch.
