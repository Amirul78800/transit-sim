Calculation Formulas & Standard References
1. Mobile Base Mass, UDL & Caster Bias EN 1991-1-1 §6.3 · EN 1991-3 / ASCE 7 · TR34

Towed wheeled mobile bases traverse lift sills and floor joints faster than creeping skates, requiring a higher dynamic factor (Φ = 1.25–1.40). Twin-wheel casters halve Mode 1 surface load per wheel while sharing one Mode 2 fork footprint.

Mgross = mpay + mframe + Ncmcaster,   UDL = WgrossLbaseWbase (1.1)
Fcaster,peak = Wgross · max(1Nc, Bias) · Φ,   Fwheel,peak = Fcaster,peakwcaster (1.2)
2. Mode 1 — Surface Contact Stress Hertz Line Contact · Gent (1958) · BS 8110-1 Cl. 5.2.3.4

Elastomeric wheel flattening is computed from Shore A hardness via Gent’s equation and Hertzian line contact; peak contact stress is 27.3% above mean pressure.

b = √4 F Rπ Lw E*,   pmax = 1.273 F2b Lw ≤ min(Pallow, 0.4fcu) (2.1)
3. Mode 2 — Slab Punching Shear BS 8110-1:1997 Cl. 3.7.7 · EN 1992-1-1 Cl. 6.4

Checks diagonal slab shear through depth d under ULS load γ·Fcaster,peak at the combined caster fork perimeter u0 and outer control perimeter u1.

VEd = γ · Fcaster,peak,   u1 = u0 + 12d (BS 8110)  |  u0 + 4πd (EC2) (3.1)
4. Mode 3 — Slab Bending & Plate Rigidity BS 8110-1 Cl. 3.5.2.2 · Method B · AISC Design Guide 1

Sweeps all caster axle positions across span L using Method B and divides peak moment by effective strip width be against the UDL rating benchmark qratingL2/8.

Mimp = Mmaxbe ≤ qratingL28,   treq = c√3pσallow (4.1)
