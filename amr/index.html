Calculation Formulas & Standard References
1. Load Accumulation, UDL & 3-Point Rocking EN 1991-1-1 §6.3 · EN 1991-3 · TR34 / ASTM E1155

Gravity loads accumulate top-down from payload to floor contact. Under creeping transit (≤ 5 m/min), inertial acceleration is modest (Φ = 1.20), whereas local 2–5 mm floor dips (TR34) unload one corner of rigid 4-point skates, shifting 35% of skate load onto the heaviest corner wheel or coaxial fork.

Mgross = mpay + mbase + 2mpow + Nfol(mfol + madd) (1.1)
UDL = Mgross · g / 1000Lbase · Wbase ≤ qrating (1.2)
Fskate,peak = Wgross · max(1N, Bias) · Φ1.20 (1.3)
Fwheel,peak = 0.35 · Fskate,peakwfork (4w / 4-fork coax)  |  Fskate,peak⌊0.5nw⌋ (>4w inline) (1.4)
2. Mode 1 — Surface Contact Stress (0–5 mm Skin) Hertz Line Contact · Gent (1958) / ASTM D2240 · BS 8110-1 Cl. 5.2.3.4

Surface bearing checks only the top floor finish and concrete skin directly beneath a wheel or rigid spreader plate. Elastomeric wheel flattening is computed from Shore A hardness via Gent’s relation and Hertzian line contact; peak semi-elliptic contact stress is 27.3% above mean pressure.

EPU = 0.0981 (56 + 7.62336 S)0.137505 (254 − 2.54 S) [MPa],   1E* = 1 − νw2Ew + 1 − νc2Ec (2.1)

pmean = FA,   pmax = 1.273 pmean ≤ min(Pallow, 0.4 fcu) (plate: pmax = pmean) (2.3)
3. Mode 2 — Slab Punching Shear (Full Depth d) BS 8110-1:1997 Cl. 3.7.7 · EN 1992-1-1 Cl. 6.4 · EN 1991-1-1

Unlike surface stress, punching shear checks whether an unreinforced concrete slab fails along a diagonal shear cone through its effective depth d. Coaxial wheel pairs share one combined perimeter u0; checks apply ULS partial load factors (γf = 1.60 BS 8110, γQ = 1.50 EC2) at both u0 and the outer control perimeter u1.

VEd = γ · Fcluster,   u0 = 2(a1 + a2),   vEd,0 = β VEdu0 d (3.1)
BS 8110 (1.5d): u1 = u0 + 12d,   vc = 0.791.25(100ρl)1/3(400d)1/4(fcu25)1/3,   vmax = min(0.8√fcu, 5) (3.2)
EC2 (2.0d): u1 = u0 + 4πd,   vRd,c = max(0.12 k(100ρl fck)1/3, 0.035 k3/2fck1/2),   vRd,max = 0.4νfcd (3.3)
4. Mode 3 — Slab Bending & Plate Rigidity BS 8110-1 Cl. 3.5.2.2 · Method B · AISC Design Guide 1

Concentrated skate loads crossing a beam bay of span L are swept longitudinally and transversely using Method B to find peak moment Mmax at zero shear, then dispersed over effective strip width be against the UDL rating benchmark. Spreader plates must exceed thickness treq to prevent cantilever edge curling.

MUDL,bench = qrating · L28,   be = min(Wbase, bload + 2.4ak(1 − akL)) (single-rail strip) (4.1)
RB = Σ Pi aiL,   Mmax = RA ak − Σai<ak Pi(ak − ai),   Mimp = Mmaxbe (4.2)
c = Bcap − Wskate2,   treq = c √3 pσallow (4.3)
